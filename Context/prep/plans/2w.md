# 2-week coding plan (interview is close)

Pattern-first and triage-heavy: you have deep engineering experience but less recent
LeetCode; the goal is to reactivate patterns fast, not to grind volume. Each session:
**20-min template review -> 1 easy warm-up -> 1-2 mediums in the priority patterns.**

- **Day 1-2** — arrays-hashing, two-pointers (warm-ups + 2 mediums each). Review templates.
- **Day 3-4** — sliding-window, stack. Time yourself: medium in <= 25 min.
- **Day 5-6** — trees-bfs-dfs, graph-bfs-dfs. Say the DecisionCanvas topo-sort out loud.
- **Day 7** — rest / re-do the 3 problems that felt worst.
- **Day 8-9** — graph-topo, dp-1d (the two highest-leverage patterns for you).
- **Day 10-11** — heap, intervals, binary-search.
- **Day 12** — the role's practical pattern (log-parsing or state-machine) + 1 debug exercise.
- **Day 13** — full mock: 1 medium + 1 medium, 45 min, talk while coding.
- **Day 14** — light review of talking-points; sleep.

Track progress with the checklist in coding_pack.md. Skip hards unless the format demands them.
