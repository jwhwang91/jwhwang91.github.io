# 4-week coding plan (default)

Pattern-first for an experience-strong / LC-weaker candidate. Each session:
**20-min template review -> 1 easy warm-up -> 1-2 mediums**, ~5 sessions/week.

- **Week 1 — foundations:** arrays-hashing, two-pointers, sliding-window, stack, strings.
  One easy + one medium per pattern; rebuild speed and the "which pattern is this" reflex.
- **Week 2 — trees & graphs:** trees-bfs-dfs, graph-bfs-dfs, graph-topo. Rehearse the
  Kahn's-topo-sort -> DecisionCanvas talking-point until it is automatic.
- **Week 3 — dp & the rest:** dp-1d, heap, intervals, binary-search, matrix, design.
  Add your first 2-3 hards here if the format is leetcode-standard.
- **Week 4 — role practical + mocks:** the role's practical pattern (log-parsing for
  validation, state-machine for embedded), 2 debug/code-review exercises, and 2-3 timed
  mock rounds (45 min, narrate). Re-do your weakest 5 problems.

Aim for ~2 mediums solved cleanly per session by week 3. Depth over volume.
